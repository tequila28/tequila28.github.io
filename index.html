Biography

I am a master's student at Peking University. I received my undergraduate degree from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications.

My research focuses on large language models and multimodal understanding. I am especially interested in post-training methods, including rejection sampling fine-tuning and reinforcement learning, as well as self-evolution mechanisms for large models. I also work on multimodal large models for scene understanding and recognition.
